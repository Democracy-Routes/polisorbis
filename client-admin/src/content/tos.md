## What the service is

Polis is a tool for public consultation. An organizer opens a conversation on a question and seeds it with short statements. Participants vote on statements (agree, disagree or pass) and may write their own. The service computes which statements find agreement across groups of participants and shows the result. Participation is anonymous; see the [privacy notice](/privacy).

## Taking part

- You may vote and write statements in any conversation whose link you have, unless the organizer has restricted it.
- Statements must be about the question of the conversation. Do not write personal data about other people, insults, threats, discriminatory content, advertising, or anything unlawful.
- Organizers moderate statements and may reject or remove any statement without giving a reason. A rejected statement is not shown to other participants.
- Do not try to vote more than once per person, use automated tools to vote or submit statements, or interfere with the service.

## Organizer accounts

The operator decides who may open an organizer account. Organizers are responsible for the conversations they create: their title, description, seed statements and the moderation of what participants write. Organizers must not use the service to collect personal data from participants.

## The operator's role

The operator may close a conversation, which stops new votes; delete a conversation and its data; remove content; and suspend accounts that break these terms. The operator may change these terms; the version shown on this page applies.

## Content

Statements written by participants and organizers remain theirs, but by submitting a statement you allow the operator to show it in the conversation and its results, and to include it in exports and reports about the conversation, in anonymized form.
